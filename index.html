Privacy Policy

Jap Counter is a devotional Naam Jap counting application developed by ShubPri.

Information We Collect

Jap Counter is designed to allow users to track their Naam Jap practice. Counter data, history, goals and related devotional progress may be stored locally on the user's device.

Internet and License Verification

The app may connect to our server for license and app-access verification. This verification is used to manage application access and does not require the user to provide personal information such as name, phone number or address.

Backup and Restore

The app provides backup and restore functionality. Backup data is controlled by the user and is not automatically shared with us.

Payments and Support

If a user voluntarily chooses to support the developer through the provided UPI option, the payment is processed through the user's chosen payment application or payment provider. Jap Counter does not collect or store the user's banking credentials, card details or UPI PIN.

Permissions

The app may request device permissions only when required for specific functionality, such as notifications, audio playback or other app features.

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services for application infrastructure, license verification and related technical services. These services may process limited technical information required to provide their functionality.

Children's Privacy

Jap Counter does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application and its supporting services.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions regarding Jap Counter, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on Google Play.
